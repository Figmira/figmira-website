OUR HORIZON

Make the future understandable.
Then help build it.

I never believed I could build something like Figmira. Then AI made me a little delusional, in the best way. It let me dream past what I thought was possible, and I figured the least I could do was share that. So Figmira follows curiosity wherever it leads: research on the forces shaping tomorrow, apps that make everyday life easier, tools for the games we love, and experiments that might not work at all. If it sparks our imagination, it belongs here.

Iso, Founder
