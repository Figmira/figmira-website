## The quarter in numbers

From April to June 2026, SK hynix sold **$57.1 billion** of memory chips, 257% more than the same months a year earlier. Operating profit was **$43.6 billion**, 557% more than a year earlier ([SK hynix](https://news.skhynix.com/en/q2-2026-business-results/)).

<div id="sec-expect" class="fx-anchor"></div>

## Expectations vs. reality

Analysts expected about **$46 billion** of operating profit. SK hynix delivered $43.6 billion, the best quarter in its history, and [the stock still fell about 9%](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/sk-hynix-q2-2026-slides-record-revenue-76-operating-margin-93CH-4818489) after the results ([Quartz](https://qz.com/sk-hynix-q2-2026-earnings-record-profit-misses-estimates-072926)). When a stock is priced for perfection, "amazing" can be a disappointment.

SK hynix started mass shipments of HBM4, its newest AI memory, during the quarter and plans to ramp it up through the rest of the year. It also signed long-term supply deals with about 10 major customers and is adding capacity, including a new cleanroom at its Yongin site in early 2027 ([SK hynix](https://news.skhynix.com/en/q2-2026-business-results/)).

The next results, for July to September, are due in October. Analysts expect about **$67.8B** in sales and **$53.4B** in operating profit ([Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/09/25/samsung-sk-hynix-q3-earnings-to-test-durability-of-memory)).
